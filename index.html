Munga Clipper

by Munga Tech

Munga Clipper is a personal desktop tool. It edits short gaming clips on the developer's own computer and uploads them to the developer's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

It uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only posts after the account owner authorises it.
