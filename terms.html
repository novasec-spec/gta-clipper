Munga Clipper is a personal, non-commercial tool run by the creator of Munga Tech (Kenya). It edits short video clips on the developer's own computer and uploads them to the developer's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Only the developer uses Munga Clipper, and only with accounts the developer owns. No one else can sign up, log in or post through it.

3. Content

Munga Clipper only uploads videos the developer has the right to publish: their own recordings and commentary, official publisher material used within the publisher's policies, footage from creators whose clipping programs allow it, and Creative Commons videos with attribution. Each post credits the original source. All content follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
